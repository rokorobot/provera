# PROVERA 1.0 — WORDS (CORE)

This file contains the **core vocabulary of PROVERA 1.0**.

The core lexicon represents the **minimum set of words required for everyday communication**.  
Together with `WORDS_EXTENDED.md`, it forms the complete PROVERA 1.0 vocabulary.

The vocabulary is organized **by semantic domain**, not alphabetically.

> **Note:** Verbs are listed in their **present base form**. Past and future are formed regularly with **-tun** and **-tel** (see `GRAMMAR.md`).

---
