# PROVERA 1.0

**Status:** Closed & Stable

PROVERA is a modern constructed language designed to be **used**, not endlessly redesigned.

It exists to serve creators who need a **ready-to-use linguistic system** for fiction, games, film, AI narratives, or worldbuilding — without becoming linguists themselves.

---

## What PROVERA Is

PROVERA is:

* a **complete and closed language system (version 1.0)**
* focused on **everyday speech**, not ornament or linguistic exhibition
* built around **high‑frequency language domains**
* expandable only through **versioned updates** (1.1, 1.2…)

The language prioritizes:

* clarity over complexity
* consistency over cleverness
* usability over maximalism

---

## What PROVERA Is NOT

PROVERA is not:

* a fantasy decoration language
* a linguistic experiment without structure
* a world auxiliary language (Esperanto‑style)
* a constantly changing sandbox

Once closed, PROVERA 1.0 is **stable**.

---

## Design Philosophy

### 1. Closed Core

PROVERA 1.0 is finished.
Its grammar and core vocabulary do not change.

All future development happens through:

* new minor versions (1.1, 1.2…)
* optional extensions or stylistic layers

This guarantees stability for users and creators.

---

### 2. Frequency‑First Vocabulary

The language was built by expanding the **most frequently used semantic domains first**:

* core verbs
* daily actions
* space & movement
* time & frequency
* social interaction
* family & relationships

This allows natural communication with a relatively small vocabulary.

---

### 3. Source Diversity

Lexical roots are drawn from multiple language families with **balanced distribution**:

* Romance / Latin languages
* Japanese
* Other Asian languages
* Non‑Indo‑European and internal abstractions

No single language dominates the sound or structure.

---

### 4. Minimal Grammar

PROVERA uses:

* regular morphology
* no irregular verbs
* predictable pluralization
* simple and transparent syntax

Complexity is achieved through **combination**, not exception.

---

## Language Layers

PROVERA contains clearly separated registers:

* **Everyday Speech** – natural dialogue, emotions, social phrases
* **Formal / Technical Register** – instructions, reports, system messages
* **Informal / Vulgar Layer (optional)** – emotional stress, realism in dialogue

Users may choose which layers to employ.

---

## Intended Use Cases

PROVERA is well suited for:

* 🎬 **Film & TV (especially sci‑fi)** – crew dialogue, AI systems, future cultures
* 🎮 **Games & Interactive Media** – NPC dialogue, UI text, fictional societies
* 📖 **Fiction & Worldbuilding** – background language, short embedded dialogue
* 🤖 **AI / System Narratives** – readable but non‑English logic and tone

PROVERA is a tool for creators who want language **without distraction**.

## Contributions

PROVERA welcomes proposals, but **does not accept direct changes** to version 1.0.

* New words and ideas are submitted as proposals
* Accepted proposals appear only in future versions
* Closed releases are never modified

This preserves linguistic integrity.
