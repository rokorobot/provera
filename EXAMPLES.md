# PROVERA 1.0 — EXAMPLES

This document provides **practical usage examples** of PROVERA 1.0.

The examples are intended for:
- writers and screenwriters
- game designers
- learners who want to see the language in action

Translations are provided for clarity.

---

## 1️⃣ Basic Conversation

**Sal!**  
Hi!

**Sal Tu! Come tu so?**  
Hello! How are you?

**Yo so hm. Grata.**  
I am fine. Thanks.

**Fin sal.**  
Goodbye.

---

## 2️⃣ Family Dialogue

**Mata cura anaka.**  
The mother takes care of the daughter.

**Pato resta dom.**  
The father stays at home.

**Genan cura anakan.**  
The parents take care of the children.

**Kapago hana kon virgo.**  
The wife speaks with the husband.

---

## 3️⃣ Daily Life

**Yo domta intra dom.**  
I live inside the house.

**Yo hamta ham et glokta glok.**  
I eat food and drink.

**Tu mota ad facha.**  
You go to work.

**Yo resta calma.**  
I stay calm.

---

## 4️⃣ Work & Duty

**Vir fachata sempa.**  
The man always works.

**Tu musata facha.**  
You must work.

**Yo conta taska.**  
I continue the task.

**Toma. Resta calma.**  
Stop. Stay calm.

---

## 5️⃣ Emotions & Reactions

**Fel!**  
Happy!

**Meho…**  
Meh…

**Ugo!**  
Ugh!

**Naho.**  
No / nah.

---

## 6️⃣ Questions

**Come tu so?**  
How are you?

**Kam tu mota?**  
Where are you going?

**Quen tu veni?**  
When do you come?

---

## 7️⃣ Sci‑Fi / System Messages

**Sistema activa.**  
System active.

**Cauta. Temp alta.**  
Warning. High temperature.

**Musata resta intra dom.**  
You must remain inside.

**Signal perda.**  
Signal lost.

---

## 8️⃣ Short Narrative

**Yo mota ad dom.**  
I go home.

**Kapa et gama resta kon anakan.**  
The woman and the man stay with the children.

**Calma. Fel temp.**  
Calm. A happy moment.
