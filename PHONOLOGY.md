# PROVERA 1.0 — PHONOLOGY

This document defines the **official sound system and pronunciation rules** of PROVERA 1.0.

The phonology is designed to be:
- easy to pronounce for international speakers
- suitable for actors, voice acting, and TTS
- neutral (not strongly tied to any single natural language)

---

## 1️⃣ Phonetic Style

PROVERA phonetics are:
- **clear and open**
- slightly **rounded** rather than sharp
- optimized for **spoken dialogue**

The language avoids:
- heavy consonant clusters
- harsh or guttural sounds
- tonal distinctions

---

## 2️⃣ Vowels

### Vowel inventory

PROVERA uses **five basic vowels**:

| Letter | Pronunciation | Example |
|------|---------------|---------|
| a | /a/ as in *father* | kapa |
| e | /e/ as in *bed* | vera |
| i | /i/ as in *machine* | lakmi |
| o | /o/ as in *more* | dom |
| u | /u/ as in *rule* | lupo |

### Vowel length & stress

- Vowel length is **not phonemic** (no meaning change)
- Lengthening may be used for **emphasis only** in speech

---

## 3️⃣ Consonants

### Allowed consonants

| Letter | Pronunciation |
|------|---------------|
| p | /p/ |
| b | /b/ |
| t | /t/ |
| d | /d/ |
| k | /k/ |
| g | /g/ |
| f | /f/ |
| s | /s/ |
| z | /z/ |
| m | /m/ |
| n | /n/ |
| l | /l/ |
| r | rolled or tapped r |
| v | /v/ |
| h | /h/ |

### Notes
- **r** may be tapped or lightly rolled
- **h** is always pronounced
- No consonant softening or palatalization

---

## 4️⃣ Syllable Structure

### Allowed syllables

- **V** (a, e)
- **CV** (ka, no, lu)
- **CVC** (dom, fren)

### Restrictions

- No more than **one consonant at the start** of a syllable
- No more than **one consonant at the end**
- No consonant clusters

---

## 5️⃣ Stress Rules

- Primary stress is placed on the **first syllable** by default

Examples:
- **KA-pa**
- **LAK-mi**
- **MO-ta**

Stress may shift for emphasis in dialogue but does not change meaning.

---

## 6️⃣ Intonation

- Statements: falling intonation
- Yes/no questions: rising intonation
- Wh-questions: falling intonation

Examples:
- **Tu mota.**
- **Tu mota?**
- **Ubi tu mota?**

---

## 7️⃣ Pronunciation in Fast Speech

- Vowels may slightly reduce but remain audible
- Consonants are never dropped
- Word boundaries remain clear

PROVERA is designed to remain intelligible even at high speaking speed.

---

## 8️⃣ Recommendations for Media Use

For film, games, and TTS:
- keep pronunciation consistent across speakers
- avoid exaggerated accents
- prefer neutral international pronunciation

---

## 🔒 Status

✔ This phonology is **official and frozen for PROVERA 1.0**.

All phonetic changes are reserved for future versions only.
